Privacy when you report a dog

← Back to Report a DogHome

Lost Dogs Ayrshire is a volunteer group that helps find missing dogs in Ayrshire. This page explains what happens to the information you give us when you use our Report a Dog page to tell us about a lost, loose or found dog, or that you have seen one of the dogs we are looking for.

What we keep

  • About the dog: what you tell us, such as the dog's name (if it is yours), where and when, a description, a map pin and a photo if you add them.
  • About you: your name and your phone number, and your email address if you choose to give it.
  • If you report for the owner: your relationship to them, and the owner's name and phone number.

Your email is optional: we only use it if we can't reach you by phone. We do not ask for your address or anything else, and the page does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

If you report a dog for its owner

If you are reporting a lost dog for its owner, we also ask for your relationship to the owner and the owner's name and phone number. We use the owner's details only to phone them about their dog and ask their permission. We will not start a search until the owner has agreed. We may still post about the dog to raise awareness (see “Will my report be posted?” below). If you are the owner and someone has given us your details, they are kept and deleted in the same way as below, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Why we keep it

So that one of our coordinators can phone you (or email you, if you gave an email address) about the dog, and so we can search for it or reunite it with its family. We only keep your details because you ticked the box to agree. We never sell them or use them for anything else.

Will my report be posted?

Reports are not posted automatically: a coordinator reads every report first. If we feel the information may help raise awareness, Lost Dogs Ayrshire may post about the dog on our Facebook Page (and in posters), and update the post if our followers send in sightings. We decide this case by case, and by sending a report you understand that we may do this.

Posts only ever include details about the dog, such as its photo, description and the area it was seen. Your name, phone number and email address are never included.

Who can see it

  • Only Lost Dogs Ayrshire's coordinators and admins can see your report and the contact details in it.
  • Names, phone numbers and email addresses are never shown publicly and never posted on Facebook or anywhere else.
  • Details about the dog (not about you) may be used in our search, and in posts and posters to help find the dog or its family.
  • Your report is stored securely by Google, which provides the database we use, and is protected in transit and in storage.

How long we keep it

Your report is deleted automatically 30 days after a coordinator has dealt with it, or after 60 days if it was never dealt with. Reports that are clearly not genuine may be deleted sooner.

Photos

Before a photo is sent, your phone makes it smaller and removes the hidden information cameras add (such as where it was taken). Each photo is then checked automatically by Google's image-checking service, so that inappropriate pictures can be refused. The photo is only used for that check and is not kept by the service.

Cookies

Our website does not use cookies for tracking, advertising or counting visitors. When you fill in a form, your phone keeps what you have typed until you send it or close the page, so nothing is lost if the page reloads. This stays on your phone and is needed for the form to work.

Some advice pages have videos from Facebook or YouTube. These only load if you tap Tap to play video. Facebook or YouTube may then set their own cookies, under their own privacy policies.

Stopping misuse

To stop the page being flooded with reports, we keep a scrambled code made from your internet connection for up to two days. We never store your actual internet address.

The list of dogs on the page

The list shows only each dog's photo, name, area and status. No owner's or reporter's details are ever shown.

Requests to Drone Search & Rescue Ayrshire

Drone Search & Rescue Ayrshire (DSRA) is an initiative of Lost Dogs Ayrshire. When you use Request a drone search on our DSRA page, we keep what you tell us about the search (what it is for, the police reference number and whether the person is vulnerable if it is a missing person, where and when, what the area is like and the details you give) and your name, phone number and email address.

We use it so a drone pilot can assess the request and contact you. If the request is about a missing person, the pilot will speak with Police Scotland about it and may share the details of your request with them, as Police Scotland lead those searches. If we get the go-ahead, the pilot may ask you for a photo of the person and a description of what they were last wearing. These are only used for that search.

Requests are seen only by our admins and the DSRA team, never shown publicly, and are deleted in the same way as reports: 30 days after they are dealt with, or after 60 days if they were never dealt with. To have a request deleted sooner, email lostdogsayrshiredrones@gmail.com.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also change your mind about being contacted at any time. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

To have your report deleted sooner

Email Lost Dogs Ayrshire at lostdogsayrshire@gmail.com, or send a message to the Lost Dogs Ayrshire Facebook Page. Tell us roughly when you sent the report and the phone number you gave, and we will delete it and let you know.
