Privacy Policy for the Lost Dogs Ayrshire app and reporting page

Lost Dogs Ayrshire is a volunteer group that helps find missing dogs in Ayrshire. Our app is used only by our approved volunteers; our reporting page can be used by anyone. This page explains what information we keep, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we keep

  • Volunteers: your name, email address and sign-in, your role in the group, the town or village you set off from (not your address), any search equipment and serial numbers you list, your signed volunteer agreement and emergency contact, which procedures (SOPs) you have read, and your replies when we ask who is available for a search.
  • Missing dogs: details of dogs we are searching for (photos, description, where and when last seen, sightings and updates). Closed cases are deleted automatically 30 days after they close.
  • Phones: a notification code for each phone that has alerts switched on, so we can send search alerts.

Reporting a dog on our website

Anyone can use our reporting page to tell us about a lost, loose or found dog, or a sighting of a dog we are looking for.

  • What we keep: what you tell us about the dog (where, when, a description, a map pin and a photo if you add them), your name and your phone number.
  • Why: so a coordinator can phone you about the dog and organise a search or reunite it with its family. We only keep it because you ticked the box to agree.
  • Who can see it: only our coordinators and admins, in our app. Your name and phone number are never shown publicly or posted on Facebook. Details of the dog (not you) may be added to the dog's record for our volunteers.
  • How long: your report is deleted automatically 30 days after a coordinator has dealt with it, or after 60 days if it was never dealt with.
  • Stopping misuse: to stop floods of reports, we keep a scrambled code made from your connection (not the address itself) for up to two days.
  • The list of dogs on the reporting page shows only each dog's photo, name, area and status.

To have a report deleted sooner, phone us on 07715 950588 or message our Facebook Page.

Why we keep it

Only to organise searches for missing dogs safely: to alert volunteers, see who is available and nearby, and know who to contact in an emergency. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Volunteers see dog information and their own details. Our admin team can see volunteers’ profiles, availability replies and signed agreements. The information is stored securely with Google Firebase.

Facebook

Our coordinators can use the app to post a missing, loose or found dog poster and its text to the Lost Dogs Ayrshire Facebook Page. These posts contain dog information only; the app does not post volunteers’ personal information to Facebook. The app does not collect any information from Facebook users.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

How to have your information deleted

  1. Contact Lost Dogs Ayrshire on 07715 950588, or send a message to the Lost Dogs Ayrshire Facebook Page, asking for your app information to be deleted.
  2. An admin will remove your access and then delete your profile, equipment list, signed agreement, procedure sign-offs, availability replies, phone notification codes and sign-in.
  3. We will confirm once it is done. Please return any equipment loaned to you by the group first.
